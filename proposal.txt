Project Title: QuickBank – Supermarket Point of Sale (POS) System

Description:
I will create a complete supermarket checkout system called "QuickBank" that allows a cashier to scan or enter products, 
track a customer's order in real time, apply discounts (including buy-one-get-one-half-off), 
calculate tax, accept payment, give change, print a receipt, and log all sales for end-of-day reporting.

Real-World Use:
This program directly solves the problem my uncle (and thousands of small store owners) face: 
moving from paper receipts to a fast, reliable digital checkout system. 
It can be used in real grocery stores, convenience shops, or markets.

Core Functions (each will do one thing):
- load_products(filename) → dict
- add_to_cart(cart, product_id, quantity)
- apply_bogo_discount(cart, product_id)
- calculate_subtotal(cart)
- calculate_tax(subtotal, rate=0.06)
- calculate_change(payment, total)
- print_receipt(cart, subtotal, tax, total, change)
- log_transaction(transaction_data)
- generate_daily_report()

Testable Functions (I will write pytest tests for at least these):
- test_calculate_subtotal()
- test_apply_bogo_discount()
- test_calculate_tax()
- test_calculate_change()
